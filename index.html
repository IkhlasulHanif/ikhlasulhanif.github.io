Indonesians usually don't have family names, so just call me Hanif.

I'm a Ph.D. student in Natural Language Processing at MBZUAI, working on language models that are efficient, safe, and useful beyond English.

I'm part of KotoLab, advised by Prof. Fajri Koto and Prof. Iryna Gurevych, and currently a visiting researcher at SUTD with Wenxuan Zhang's group. My research covers efficient large language models, safety and security, and multilingual NLP for low-resource languages.
